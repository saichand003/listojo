03 Rules for listings

You must

  • Have the legal right to list the property.
  • Describe it accurately, including price, availability, size, and condition.
  • Use photos of the actual property that you own or have permission to use.
  • Keep listings current, and mark them rented, sold, or removed when no longer available.
  • Comply with all applicable landlord-tenant, licensing, and advertising laws.

You must not

  • Post a property that does not exist, is not available, or that you have no right to offer.
  • Post bait listings, duplicate listings, or listings whose real purpose is to collect leads.
  • Publish discriminatory preferences or restrictions. See section 4.
  • Ask for deposits, application fees, or wire transfers before a prospective tenant has seen the property and signed an agreement.
  • Scrape, crawl, or bulk-copy listings, or use bots against the site.
  • Post someone else's personal information, or content you do not have rights to.
  • Use Listojo to spam, harass, defraud, or distribute malware.

Listojo supports equal housing opportunity. Federal law prohibits statements of preference, limitation, or discrimination in housing advertising based on race, color, religion, sex, disability, familial status, or national origin. Texas and local law may protect further characteristics.

This applies to listing text as much as to formal policy. We remove listings that violate it and may suspend the accounts behind them. If you believe a listing is discriminatory, report it to us.
