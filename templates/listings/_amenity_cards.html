{% comment %} The two amenity catalogues for one place. `place` is a Listing or a Community. Both carry `community_amenities` and `in_unit_amenities` on their own tables and inherit AmenityDisplayMixin, which also decides the headings — see that mixin for why the first one is conditional. `card_style` says where the cards sit. With nothing passed they are `.card` blocks on the page background, which is what both detail pages want; a caller that nests them inside another card passes a tinted panel style instead. `chip_bg` inverts with it: a chip has to contrast with whatever is behind it, and a tinted panel is the same colour the chips use by default. `layout="tiles"` swaps the chip rows for the reference design's grid of icon tiles under an open heading with a count.
