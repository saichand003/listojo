About Listojo

Renting in DFW shouldn't cost a broker's fee

Listojo is a Dallas–Fort Worth rental marketplace built for the people the big portals leave out — independent landlords with a handful of units, and renters tired of paying to be matched with a home.

{# ── Why ── #}

01 Why we built this

If you own one or two rental units in DFW, your options are bad. The major listing portals are priced for property management companies, not for someone renting out a single townhouse in Irving. So most small landlords end up on Craigslist or a Facebook group — free, but no verification, no structure, and an inbox full of spam.

Renters get the mirror image of that problem: listings scattered across a dozen places, half of them stale, most of them impossible to compare on the things that actually matter once you live there.

{# ── Differentiators ── #}

02 What makes Listojo different

🏷️

Free to list

No listing fees, no agency commission, no paywall between you and an interested renter. Post a unit in a few minutes.

⭐

Match scores, not just filters

Tell us what matters — budget, bedrooms, commute, pets, parking — and every listing gets scored against it, with the reasons shown.

🚶

Neighborhood data built in

Walk, transit and bike scores on listings, so you know what life around the address is actually like before you tour it.

💬

Talk to the owner directly

Message the person who owns the unit. No lead resale, no call center, no middleman marking up the conversation.

{# ── Who for ── #}

03 Who Listojo is for

🔑 Independent landlords

You manage between one and five units yourself. You want them filled by a good tenant without paying a percentage of the rent to do it.

🏠 Renters and roommates

You are moving somewhere in the Metroplex and want to compare real places on real criteria, then talk to whoever holds the keys.

{# ── Coverage ── #}

04 Where we operate

Listojo is Dallas–Fort Worth first. We would rather be genuinely useful in one metro than thin across fifty, so the Metroplex is where we are putting everything until it works properly here.
